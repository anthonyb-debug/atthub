Attendance computation guide

1 · Scheduled minutes (from the shift)

Read from the shift time range: the shift length minus an unpaid break.

Scheduled = shift length − break
break = 60 min  (120 min if the shift is 12 h or longer)

OFF, NOT YET HIRED, or a blank shift = not scheduled (no minutes).

Example: 9:00 AM–6:00 PM = 9 h (540 min) − 60 = 480 scheduled minutes. In the Tracker this shows a green ✓; the Minutes column shows the number.

2 · Minutes worked

Minutes = Scheduled − (Absent + Tardy + Early Out)

Leave is planned and credited — it does not reduce Minutes. A leave day keeps its full scheduled minutes (e.g. 480).

Absent and Leave are full-day and use that day’s scheduled minutes. Tardy and Early Out are entered in minutes.

3 · Dashboard metrics

Loss = Absent + Early Out + Tardy  (Leave excluded)
Attendance % = (Scheduled − Loss) ÷ Scheduled
Shrinkage % = Loss ÷ Scheduled

Totals roll up across the current filters (month, campaign, agent). Rest days (OFF) aren’t scheduled, so they don’t affect the percentages.

5 · Tardy watchlist

An agent is flagged for a month when either threshold is hit:

Tardy minutes ≥ 60   OR   Tardy count ≥ 6 (in the month)

Worked example

Shift 9:00 AM–6:00 PM → 480 scheduled.
20 min tardy + 15 min early out → Loss = 35, Minutes = 445.
Attendance = 445 ÷ 480 = 92.7% → Tier 2 (amber).
• Full-day absent: Loss 480, Minutes 0, Attendance 0%.
• On leave: Minutes stay 480, Attendance 100% (Tier 5).
